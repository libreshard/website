The shard editor makes your own shard without writing a line of code: a grid of buttons, with as
many pages as you need, where each button presses a key, holds it, repeats it or switches between
two. Saving installs it like any other shard, and the tablet shows it right away.

## Open it

<Steps>

1. In the LibreShard window on the PC, go to the **Shards** tab and click **New shard**.

2. Give the shard a **name** and an **icon** (an emoji or a letter) for the tablet's menu, and pick
   the size of the **grid**: up to 24 columns by 24 rows.

3. Click an empty cell to add a button.

</Steps>

To change a shard later, click **Edit** on its card: shards made with the editor carry an
**Editor** badge. Your changes stay in the editor until you click **Save**, and closing with unsaved
changes asks first.

A shard made with the editor that you got from someone else (from the gallery, a web address or a
file) says **Edit a copy** instead, and the editor tells you so as it opens. **Save as copy**
creates a new shard of yours, with "(copy)" after its name, which keeps the original's author and
license. The original stays as it is, with its settings, and keeps getting its updates. From then
on the copy is yours, and **Save** changes it in place.

## Pages

The list on the left holds the pages: **Add page**, rename one by typing over its name, move it up or
down, or delete it. Buttons can open any page, go back to the previous one, or step to the next or
previous page. **Show page tabs on the tablet** puts a tab per page at the top of the shard; turn it
off if your own buttons move between pages.

## Buttons

- **Click an empty cell** to add a button, **drag** a button to move it, and pull its **corner** to
  make it span several cells.
- **Duplicate** (Ctrl+D) copies the selected button, **Delete** (or the Delete key) removes it, and
  the arrow keys move it one cell.
- **Undo** and **Redo** (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y) cover every change, and Ctrl+S saves.

### What a button does

| Choice | What happens on the tablet |
|---|---|
| **Press a key** | One tap presses the key or combination. |
| **Hold a key** | The key stays down while your finger is on the button: Shift, Alt, push to talk. |
| **Repeat while held** | Presses again and again while held, like a volume key. You choose how often (50 to 1000 ms). |
| **Toggle between two keys** | Each tap alternates: the first key, then the second. Each state has its own text, icon and colors, so you can see which one is on. |
| **Go to a page** | Shows another page of the shard, the previous one, or the next or previous page. |
| **Text only** | A title or a note on the grid: it presses nothing. |

### Keys

- **Capture**: hold the keys on your keyboard, then let go of all of them. It follows your keyboard
  layout, so a key is saved as the character it types.
- **Choose…**: a list for keys that are awkward to capture: the Windows key, media keys (play,
  volume, mute), F13 to F24, the numeric keypad.
- A combination can hold several keys after the modifiers, pressed in order. Windows Alt codes work
  that way: Alt + Num 1 + Num 6 + Num 4 types ñ. Capture them by holding Alt and typing the digits
  on the numeric keypad, or build them with **+ add a key**.

The keys panel on the right lists every key the shard uses, with the same warnings as the review
when you install a shard: system shortcuts in red, and keys this PC's keyboard doesn't have (F13 to
F24 on many Linux keyboards).

### How a button looks

- **Text**, placed at the top, middle or bottom, in three sizes.
- An **icon**: one of LibreShard's icons, an emoji, or your own **image** (PNG, JPEG, WebP or a GIF's
  first frame). Images are shrunk to 288 px and saved again as PNG, so only the pixels end up in the
  shard. The text goes over them.
- A **background image** that covers the whole button, under its icon and text.
- The **button color** and the **text color**.

## The Look tab

**Look**, next to **Button** on the right, sets the style of the whole shard:

- **Theme**: LibreShard, Light, High contrast, OLED black or Key pad (black, with images to the
  edge of each button, like a hardware key pad). Choosing a theme clears your own settings below;
  Undo brings them back.
- **Background**: the theme's, a color, a gradient, or an image you can darken.
- **Buttons**: corners, border, fill (gradient, flat or none), shadow, color, the color when
  pressed, and whether images keep a margin or reach the edge.
- **Page tabs**: colors for the other pages and for the selected one.
- **Spacing** between buttons, and the **text**'s color, size and font.

**Back to the theme** drops your own settings, and the editor warns when text and buttons are hard
to tell apart.

## Preview and warnings

The middle of the editor draws the shard with the same code the tablet uses. **Preview** switches
between a tablet in landscape, a tablet in portrait and a phone. The editor marks:

- buttons that still need a key or a page ("!");
- buttons smaller than 48 px at that size, hard to hit with a finger;
- keys LibreShard can't press on this PC.

## Save and share

**Save** installs the shard. Its keys are approved straight away, since you chose them yourself,
and tablets showing it reload. To share it, click **Export…** on its card: it saves a `.libreshard`
file that opens in LibreShard with a double click, on any PC.

## Bring a Stream Deck profile

<Steps>

1. In the Stream Deck app, export the profile (a `.streamDeckProfile` file).

2. In LibreShard, click **Import Stream Deck profile…** in the Shards tab, or drop the file on the
   window.

3. The profile opens in the editor as a new shard, not saved yet. Check it, change what you like,
   and click **Save**.

</Steps>

Pages, folders, button titles (with their place, size and color), images, hotkeys, hotkey switches,
media keys and page buttons come along. A summary above the warnings says how many buttons work.

- Buttons LibreShard can't run (plugin actions, typing text, opening programs or websites, multi
  actions, key sequences, Stop) keep their title and image, are marked **✕**, and say why when you
  select them. Give them an action, or delete them.
- Buttons marked **?** deserve a look: shortcuts recorded on a Mac (Cmd becomes Ctrl), and Key Logic
  buttons, of which only the single press is imported.
- A virtual Stream Deck doesn't store its size, so the grid fits the buttons it uses. Make it bigger
  in **Grid** if you want more room.

The profile stays on your PC: LibreShard only reads the file you chose.

## When a grid isn't enough

For a layout of your own, buttons that change with a setting or remember things, write a shard as
a web page ([Create a shard](/docs/create-a-shard/)), or let
[an AI assistant write it](/docs/ai-assistant/) for you.
