Shards made by the community and reviewed before they're listed. **Install in LibreShard** opens the
app, which downloads the reviewed file, checks it's exactly that file, and shows you every key the
shard can press before installing. You can also browse this list in the app: **Shards → Explore the
gallery**. Both need LibreShard 0.5 or newer.

<Gallery lang="en" />

## Publish your shard

Made a shard? List it here: add an entry with your file's address and its sha256 to the
[libreshard/shards](https://github.com/libreshard/shards#publish-your-shard) repository with a pull
request. Every shard is reviewed before it's listed (see the
[review policy](https://github.com/libreshard/shards/blob/main/POLICY.md)), usually within a week.
Paid shards can be listed too: the gallery links to your shop.
