## How it works

<Steps>

1. **Install LibreShard on your PC.** Windows and Linux are supported; macOS is experimental.

2. **Scan the QR code with your tablet.** The panel opens in its browser: there's no app to install on the tablet.

3. **Tap.** The PC presses the keys for you, in the app you're using.

</Steps>

## Why LibreShard

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="No vendor lock-in" icon="mobile-android">
		Reuse an old tablet or the phone you already have. Any modern browser works: Android, iPad,
		phones or another computer.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Shards" icon="puzzle">
		Each panel is a folder with a web page and a manifest. Install one by dropping a zip on the
		window, or build your own and watch the tablet reload as you save.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Safe by design" icon="padlock">
		The PC presses only the keys a shard declared **and** you approved, after a review that flags
		dangerous shortcuts. Shards can't reach the internet, your files or each other.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Only where it belongs" icon="approve-check-circle">
		Limit a shard to the apps it's meant for, so a tap never fires a shortcut into the wrong
		program.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Local and private" icon="laptop">
		No account, no telemetry. The only connection outside your network is a daily check for new
		versions, which sends nothing about you and can be turned off.
	</Card>
	<Card title="English and Spanish" icon="translate">
		The app follows your system language, or the one you pick in Settings.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## Ready to use

LibreShard comes with eight shards: **Media**, **Numpad**, **Image editing** (Photoshop, Affinity
Photo, Krita, GIMP), **Video calls** (Zoom, Teams, Meet), **Desktop** (windows and virtual
desktops), **Discord**, **OBS Studio** and **VS Code**. More are coming from the community, like
the [SC MFD](https://github.com/libreshard/libreshard-sc-mfd) panels for Star Citizen.
