LibreShard is free and open source, and it will stay that way: nothing is locked behind a payment,
there are no ads and no account. If it's useful to you, you can support its development. It's
entirely optional.

Donations go to LibreShard's maintainer and pay for the time it takes: new features, fixes,
reviewing gallery shards, testing on real devices, and the website. They don't buy a better place
in the gallery or a say over the roadmap, and LibreShard keeps working the same whether you donate
or not.

## Other ways to help

- **Make a shard** and [publish it in the gallery](/gallery/#publish-your-shard), or
  [sell it](/docs/sell-a-shard/).
- **Report a bug** or suggest something: Settings → **Report a bug** in the app, or the
  [issues](https://github.com/libreshard/libreshard/issues).
- **Tell people** who could use it: streamers, artists, players, anyone with a spare tablet.
- **Contribute** code, docs or translations: see [Community](/community/).
